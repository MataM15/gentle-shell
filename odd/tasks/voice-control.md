# Gentle Voice control: audible cues, spoken commands, overlay, wake word

Base: `personal/main` @ `e815d8936`. Worktree `gentle-shell-worktrees/voice-control`, branch `feat/voice-control`. Roadmap agreed with the owner on 2026-11-12 (phases 0-7 below); open owner decision noted in S7.

## Specs

- **S1 — Audible microphone cues.** Owner: "Quiere un sonido claro cuando el micrófono se activa y otro cuando se corta, que se escuche por los parlantes (hoy no lo oye), para saber sin mirar la pantalla si lo está escuchando." Today cues play through WSLg PulseAudio (default sink, 160 ms, 0.25 gain) while replies play through Windows (PowerShell); the log shows every cue `done`, so the route and level are the problem. Cues must play on the same Windows output the reply voice uses, be clearly louder and longer, and open/close must be unmistakably different. Latency stays low (a warm player); PulseAudio remains the fallback when the Windows path is unavailable.
- **S2 — A dictated turn reopens the mic.** Evidence 2026-11-12 18:37-18:43Z (session `01a1cd43…`): owner used Alt+M dictation, the transcript went to the editor, owner sent it with Enter (`input source=interactive spokenSend=false`), so after the reading `mic.reopen skipped reason=no-spoken-turn`. Owner: "Hoy el micrófono no se abrió solo; hay que revisar por qué." A turn whose text came from a dictation counts as spoken for the reopen.
- **S3 — Spanish fixed for dictation.** Owner: "el dictado tomó «sí, siguiente» como inglés y lo transcribió «And they're». Quiere el idioma fijo en español". Parakeet v3 auto-detects language and cannot be forced; measured in T2 (L9): adopt Whisper base int8 with language "es" after a short real-mic A/B with the owner; switch only once the keyword spotter covers "Gentle Voice off" (S4/S7), since a Spanish-pinned model cannot read it.
- **S4 — "Gentle Voice off" by voice.** Owner: "decir «Gentle Voice off» para apagar todo (micrófono y lectura en voz alta), sin comandos escritos." Only a whole utterance that is the command counts; the phrase inside a sentence is sent as text. The command is never sent to the model.
- **S5 — Big overlay with a waveform.** Owner: "Quiere una ventana o aviso grande en el centro de la pantalla (idealmente con una onda de audio mientras habla)." A Windows topmost, click-through, semi-transparent window launched by Gentle Shell (PowerShell), showing state (listening / transcribing), the live mic level as a wave, the session that is listening, and the active language. Requires the worker to publish audio levels. While a reply is read aloud the wave is real too (L16): it follows the loudness the reading already publishes for other consumers such as the owner's LED lights (`lib/voice-speech-events.ts`, one value per 50 ms of audio the player actually started).
- **S6 — One listener for all sessions.** With many Pi sessions in herdr, one process owns the mic, wake word, cues and overlay; dictation goes to the focused session.
- **S7 — "Gentle Voice on" by voice.** Owner: "decir «Gentle Voice on» para empezar a escuchar". Local keyword spotting (sherpa-onnx KeywordSpotter ships in the installed runtime; a small KWS model must be pinned). Test the owner's pronunciation before committing. Must not hear its own reading. The same English keyword spotter (pin candidate sherpa-onnx-kws-zipformer-gigaspeech-3.3M-2024-01-01, Apache-2.0) also catches "Gentle Voice off" once dictation is Spanish-pinned (L9, option A); measure false triggers on Spanish speech. Owner decision (L8): after "off" it stays ASLEEP: mic, readings and dictation off, only the local keyword spotter keeps listening for "Gentle Voice on"; the overlay/status must show the asleep state.
- **S8 — Navigate herdr agents by voice.** Owner: "si el día de mañana, por ejemplo, quiero navegar desde Header mediante vos entre las diferentes agentes que están activos o levantados, me gustaría poder hacerlo". Fixed command list over `herdr agent list/focus`; sending a prompt to another agent asks for confirmation.
- **S9 — Deliberate English mode.** Owner: "más adelante, un modo inglés que se active a propósito para practicar." Switched on and off on purpose only, never auto-detected; the overlay shows it.
- **S11 — Confirmation window in conversation mode.** Owner: "me quedo callado, ¿no? Que se quede el texto un segundo. Y si no confirme, bien, si lo envío o no, porque quizá lo quiero borrar." After a hands-free utterance, the transcript shows in the overlay with a short countdown (~3 s): silence sends it; the whole utterance "cancel" discards it. Also: after a reading, if the owner does not answer, the mic falls to asleep and "Gentle Voice on" resumes conversation with auto-send. Out of scope by owner agreement: spoken line breaks, lists or editing (Alt+M + keyboard covers them); "Gentle send" dropped.
- **S12 — Only the focused session reads aloud.** Owner: "que esperar que la gente me hable cuando está enfocado en la sesión. Bien, porque yo he escuchado que a veces me habla, cuando estoy en otra sesión, me habla desde otra sesión, entonces bueno no puedo prestar atención así." A reply from a session that is not focused in herdr is held, not read; it is read when the owner focuses that session, if it settled less than 10 minutes ago; older ones are not read (the text stays on screen). Owner choice: "Guardar y leer al enfocar (Recomendado)". Done first in the per-session voice, later moved to the listener.
- **S13 — Echo cancellation and barge-in (inspired by runboubou/boubou).** Owner: "Si.. quizas podriamos levantar quizas algun modelito en local que nos permita hacer lo mismo o parecido.. a ver que nos sirve.. e integrarlo para nosotros". Research first: which local components (echo cancellation, voice activity detection) let the mic stay open while replies play through the speakers without hearing itself, and let the owner interrupt a reading by speaking. Fully local; no new reasoning model (Pi stays the brain). Decided after the real measurements (L20): a reading is interrupted **only by a spoken command**, never by speech in general, background noise or another person talking; the owner can talk about anything else while a reply is read and the reading goes on. Owner: "no debería interrumpir la interferir en la redacción. Salvo que sea por comando". When the command is heard the reading stops and the microphone opens for the owner ("Y ahí que me tome el micrófono, bien, para que sepa que yo lo estoy interrumpiendo"). So there is no level-gate barge-in and no echo-canceller dependency; the keyword spotter of S7 listens for that command while a reply is read, and it must not be triggered by the reading itself.
- **S14 — Commands are heard whenever voice is on.** Owner, answering whether the command detector should keep listening while voice is on: "Sí, siempre escuchando comandos (Recomendado)" — the microphone stays open for the local keyword spotter alone while voice is on, between turns and during a reading, so "Gentle Voice off" (and later the T11 cut command) works at any moment; nothing is recorded or transcribed outside a capture. A reading that itself says a command phrase must not trigger it (L21). Every spotter hit, accepted or rejected, leaves one log line with the command id and the reason (never audio), so a failed real attempt can be explained.
- **S10 — Safety rules (all phases).** Owner: "evaluando situaciones también, por ejemplo, que pasaría si estoy hablando y por ejemplo, no sé, algo y se activa, o no se activa, cuestiones de seguridad". Voice never runs free text as a command (fixed list only); nothing destructive or publishing by voice; every activation is visible and audible; false activations close on silence and are logged; Alt+M always works as fallback; audio stays local and is not stored. Development probes stay near-silent (peak at most 20 of 32767).
